Our story

One group, many destinations

Artius Ltd was founded on a belief that hospitality is a craft — equal parts design, food, and the human touch.

What began as a single venture has grown into a family of distinctive places. Along the way we learned that owning and operating our own venues makes us better partners to others — because we live the same challenges, every shift, every day.

Today, Artius creates and runs its own hospitality brands while offering management and consultancy to partners who want the same rigour and creativity behind their own concepts.

A message from our founder

Built with intent

"Every Artius venture starts with a simple question: how do we want people to feel when they walk in?"

That question shapes everything — the light, the music, the menu, the welcome at the door. I started Artius because I believe hospitality, done with intent, can change someone's whole day. We are not interested in opening places quickly; we are interested in opening places that matter, and then looking after them as if our name is on the door — because it is.

To everyone who has shared a table, joined our team, or trusted us with their brand: thank you. This is only the beginning.

— Ashik Kaisar

Founder & Managing Director, Artius Ltd
